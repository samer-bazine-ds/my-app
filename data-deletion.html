Request deletion

To request deletion of information processed by PagePilot, email elbahibazine@gmail.com with the subject PagePilot Data Deletion Request.

Include enough information to identify the relevant Facebook Page interaction, such as:

  • The Facebook Page name.
  • A link to the post or comment, if available.
  • The approximate date of the interaction.
  • A description of the information you want deleted.

Do not send passwords, access tokens or government identity documents.

What happens next

The request will be verified and applicable PagePilot records stored on the local system will be deleted. If the request concerns content published on Facebook, the Page administrator will remove content they control through Facebook where appropriate. Requests are normally completed within 30 days, subject to legal or security retention requirements.

Disconnecting Facebook

An administrator may also remove the application's Facebook permissions from Facebook's Business Integrations settings and delete the local PagePilot database and credentials.
