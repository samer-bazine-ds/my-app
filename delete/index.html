PagePilot User Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of data processed by PagePilot, email elbahibazine@gmail.com with the subject "PagePilot Data Deletion Request".

Include the Facebook Page name and, when available, a link to the relevant post or comment. Do not include passwords or access tokens.

We will verify the request and delete applicable PagePilot records within 30 days, unless retention is required for legal or security reasons.

You can also remove PagePilot permissions from Facebook's Business Integrations settings.
