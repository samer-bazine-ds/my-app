Information used

When an authorized Page administrator connects PagePilot to a Facebook Page, the application may process Page identifiers, Page posts, media, public comments made on those posts, commenter display names or identifiers when Meta makes them available, generated reply drafts, publishing status and technical error information.

How information is used

This information is used only to manage the connected Facebook Page: create and schedule content, identify new comments, prepare replies, publish administrator-approved replies, avoid duplicate posts and display activity history.

AI processing

When AI generation is requested, relevant post or comment text may be sent to the configured Google Gemini API to generate a caption or reply. Administrators should review generated content before publishing. PagePilot does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Application records and access credentials are stored on the administrator's local computer. The administrator is responsible for securing that computer, protecting access tokens and controlling access to the application. Meta and Google process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention

Local records remain until the administrator removes them or deletes the local application database. Facebook content remains subject to the administrator's actions and Meta's retention rules.

Your choices

A person may request access to or deletion of locally stored information associated with their Facebook interaction by following the Data Deletion Instructions.

Changes

This policy may be updated when PagePilot's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated.
